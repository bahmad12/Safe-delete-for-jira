Safe Delete for Jira

Find every place a group, status, or priority is used — before you delete it.

PrivacySupport

Jira's 2026 data limits mean admins have to delete configuration — and deleting blind breaks filters, boards, and workflows nobody remembers building. Safe Delete scans your instance on a schedule, precomputes where every group, status, and priority is referenced, and gives you a strict SAFE / CAUTION / UNSAFE verdict with evidence and deep links into Jira admin.

Honest by design: Jira Cloud offers no API to read automation rules, so no scanner can see inside them. Instead of pretending, Safe Delete caps verdicts at CAUTION and gives you a browser-side paste analyzer for exported rules — nothing you paste is stored or transmitted.

Runs on Atlassian: Forge compute and storage only. Zero external egress, no analytics, no trackers. This page has none either.
