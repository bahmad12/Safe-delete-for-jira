The short version

Your data never leaves Atlassian. Safe Delete is a Forge app that runs entirely on Atlassian-hosted compute and storage ("Runs on Atlassian"), makes no network calls to any non-Atlassian service, and includes no analytics or tracking of any kind — in the app or on this site.

What the app processes

To build its reference reports, the app reads your Jira site's configuration metadata via read-only Jira APIs: names and identifiers of permission schemes, notification schemes, issue security levels, saved filters, dashboards, boards, workflows, priority schemes, projects and project roles, and the groups, statuses, and priorities they reference.

What the app stores

The reference index derived from that metadata, plus scan bookkeeping (timestamps, progress counters), in Forge app storage inside your own Atlassian site context. Only the most recent completed scan and any in-progress scan are kept; older scans are deleted automatically.

What the app never touches

  • Issue content, comments, or attachments (beyond an aggregate issue count for a selected status/priority).
  • Personal data beyond group names, which Jira already displays to every administrator.
  • Credentials or tokens — authentication is handled by the Forge platform.

The automation rule analyzer runs entirely in your browser: rule exports you paste are analyzed locally and are never transmitted or stored.

Data sharing and subprocessors

None. There are no third-party services, analytics providers, or subprocessors. Hosting and data residency are Atlassian's, per the Forge platform.

Data deletion

Uninstalling the app removes its Forge storage per Atlassian platform behavior. You can also simply run no further scans; stored data is configuration metadata only.
