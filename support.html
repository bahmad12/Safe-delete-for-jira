Frequently asked

Why does an unused object show CAUTION instead of SAFE?
Jira Cloud has no API for reading automation rules, so that area cannot be scanned — by us or anyone. We refuse to claim SAFE while any area is unscanned. Use the built-in paste analyzer to check your exported rules; it runs in your browser and transmits nothing.

How fresh is the data?
A full scan runs daily, and you can trigger one anytime from the app. Every report shows exactly when its scan finished.

Does the app change anything in Jira?
No. It is read-only by design; every deletion stays in your hands.
